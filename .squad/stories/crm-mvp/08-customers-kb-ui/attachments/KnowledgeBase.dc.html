Why are my SMS notifications delayed?
Mona Saeed · updated 24 August · 1,204 views · 96% found this helpful
Delivery delays on the SMS channel almost always come from the carrier queue rather than from dispatch itself. Before escalating, confirm the three checks below — they resolve roughly four in five reported delays.
1. Check the send timestamp against the delivery receipt
Open the ticket's activity log. If sent_at and delivered_at differ by more than fifteen minutes, the delay is carrier-side and the customer's own configuration is not at fault.
2. Confirm the primary route is healthy
A backlog on the primary route reroutes traffic to the secondary, which carries a lower throughput ceiling during business hours.
