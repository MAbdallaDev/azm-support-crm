Why are my SMS notifications delayed?
Body — Markdown
Delivery delays on the SMS channel almost always come from the carrier queue rather than from dispatch itself. Before escalating, confirm the three checks below.

## 1. Check the send timestamp
Open the ticket's activity log. If sent_at and delivered_at differ by more than fifteen minutes, the delay is carrier-side.
